A blueprint says what a subject is for: its core concepts, its slots (needs any one skill of a family fills, like "one general-purpose language"), optional extensions and what is out of scope. Every subject starts with an automatic draft built from its curated skill list. Gemini can re-draft one, and an administrator approves it. The faculty, admin and student views only ever suggest what a blueprint allows.
