Data Privacy Notice

UEnvision is collecting this information for academic research purposes only, under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

  • Purpose: to document the technical competencies the BSIT/BSCS curricula expect faculty to teach, for comparison against current IT/CS job market demand.
  • What is collected: the subjects you teach and the skills/teaching method you report for each. Your name and department are optional and used only to follow up on unclear responses, if needed.
  • Confidentiality: your responses are used only in aggregate for this research and are not shared outside the research team and its adviser.
  • Voluntary participation: you may decline to answer or stop at any time without consequence.
  • Questions or concerns: you may reach the UEnvision research team through the CCSS faculty coordinator.
