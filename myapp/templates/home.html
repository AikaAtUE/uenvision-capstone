Know the skills the IT job market wants, and how ready UE is for them.

UEnvision is a curriculum mapping system. It reads real job postings for IT and Computer Science roles, finds the technical skills employers ask for most, and compares them with what UE's BSIT and BSCS subjects teach. Students and alumni see their own skill gap; faculty see where the curriculum can grow.
