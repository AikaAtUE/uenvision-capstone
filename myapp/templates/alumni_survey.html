Data Privacy Notice

UEnvision is collecting this information for academic research purposes only, under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

  • Purpose: to compare the technical skills taught in the BSIT/BSCS curricula against current IT/CS job market demand, including how well they carried over into your career.
  • What is collected: your name, UE email, course, student number, year graduated, your subjects/skills, and your employment situation as you describe it.
  • Confidentiality: your responses are used only in aggregate for this research and are not shared outside the research team and its adviser.
  • Voluntary participation: you may decline to answer or stop at any time without consequence.
  • Questions or concerns: you may reach the UEnvision research team through your course adviser.
