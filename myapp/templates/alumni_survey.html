UEnvision Curriculum Survey — Fresh Graduates & Alumni

Help us compare what UE taught you with what the job market actually asked for once you graduated. This takes about 10 minutes.

Purpose of this survey: to find out which technical skills UE BSIT and BSCS graduates learned in their subjects, and how well those skills carried over into their jobs or job search. UEnvision compares this with the skills IT and Computer Science job postings ask for, to show where the curriculum matches industry needs and where it falls short.

How to answer

    {% if prefill %}
  1. Check the details from your registration under About you. Press Change if something is wrong.
    2. {% else %}
  2. Read the Data Privacy Notice and tick the box to agree.
  3. Fill in your name, email, student number, course and the year you graduated.
    4. {% endif %}
  4. Pick your course first, then your last semester at UE, and tick the subjects you took in it (Select all ticks the whole semester). You may also add subjects from your previous semesters; those are optional. For a Professional Elective, use + Add a subject that isn’t listed.
  5. For each subject, pick the skills you learned and how far you got with each one: Introduced, Practiced or Applied (the same levels faculty use; each level is explained above the skill boxes). Press × on a subject's box to remove it.
  6. Pick the skills you learned overall at UE, then answer the questions about your current job or job search.
  7. Press Submit survey. You then go straight to your dashboard to see your own skill gap results.

Data Privacy Notice

UEnvision is collecting this information for academic research purposes only, under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

  • Purpose: to compare the technical skills taught in the BSIT/BSCS curricula against current IT/CS job market demand, including how well they carried over into your career.
  • What is collected: your name, email, course, student number, year graduated, your subjects/skills, and your employment situation as you describe it.
  • Confidentiality: your responses are used only in aggregate for this research and are not shared outside the research team and its adviser.
  • Voluntary participation: you may decline to answer or stop at any time without consequence.
  • Questions or concerns: email the UEnvision research team at inquiries@uenvision.tech.
