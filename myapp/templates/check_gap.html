New to UEnvision?

UEnvision is a research project of UE CCSS. It reads real IT and Computer Science job postings, finds the skills employers ask for, and compares them with the skills taught in the BSIT and BSCS subjects.

  1. Answer a short survey. Tick the subjects you took and the skills you learned in each.
  2. Create your account with your UE email.
  3. See your results: the in-demand skills you already have, the ones you're missing, and resources to learn them.
