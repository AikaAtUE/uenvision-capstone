How to use UEnvision

UEnvision reads IT and Computer Science job postings, finds the skills employers ask for most, and compares them with what UE's BSIT and BSCS subjects teach. Below are the steps for your account{% if show_all %}, plus every other role{% endif %}.

Students and alumni

  1. Answer the curriculum survey. List the subjects you took and the skills you learned in each, with your level. If you haven't, My skill gap shows a Take the survey button.
  2. Pick the job you're aiming for. On My skill gap, choose a role under Job role I'm aiming for. Leave it on Any IT/CS role to compare with the overall top skills.
  3. Read your results. Job readiness is how many of the top skills you have at Practiced or Applied (the Your Skill Level column shows your level for each Job Skill). Skills to build lists the top skills you're missing.
  4. Find learning resources. Tick the skills you want, then press Find resources for selected skills. Check a course before paying for it.
  5. Keep it up to date. On My subjects & skills, press Edit to add subjects or raise a skill level as you improve.
{% endif %} {% if not user.is_learner or show_all %}

Faculty

  1. See the job market on the Dashboard. Choose hard or soft skills, how many top skills to show, and the source or job level. The three tabs show the top skills, how they change month to month, and the skill gap.
  2. Follow hiring trends on Job Market. Pick a source and a starting year to see postings per quarter, the skills rising and falling, job roles, top employers and cities, pay by experience level, and entry-level vs senior skills. It updates on its own as new postings are extracted.
  3. Answer the Professor Survey. Go to Data > Professor Survey and list the subjects you teach and how each skill is covered (Introduced, Practiced or Applied).
  4. Get recommendations. On Recommendations, pick one of your subjects (or all subjects) to see in-demand skills it doesn't cover yet, then find training resources for them.
  5. Look at or export the data. Data > View Data shows the job postings and skills; Export CSV downloads the table.
{% endif %} {% if user.is_administrator or show_all %}

Administrators

  1. Approve faculty. New faculty can use UEnvision as soon as they sign up. The bell shows new sign-ups. In Accounts, press Approve for real UE faculty, or Remove anyone who isn't (their survey answers are removed too).
  2. Manage accounts. Add, edit, reset the password of, or remove an account from Accounts.
  3. Update the job data. Data > Data Extraction collects new postings, pulls the skills out of them and checks them for possible ghost jobs, in one run or automatically on a schedule. Import Data uploads a CSV instead.
  4. Review survey answers. Data > Survey Responses lets you search, view and delete curriculum survey answers.
