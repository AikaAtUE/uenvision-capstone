Recommendations

After every curriculum survey, UEnvision works out each student's and each subject's recommended skills from the survey answers and the job postings, then Gemini finds learning resources for them in the background. Nothing here changes any count shown from the extracted or hard skills data sets.

Minimum respondents

A skill counts as built up in a subject once this many people (professors, students and alumni together) report it for that subject. It is used for students' assumed skills, "coming up" skills and the faculty view.
