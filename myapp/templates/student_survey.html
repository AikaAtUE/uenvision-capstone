UEnvision Curriculum Survey — Students

Help us compare what's taught in class with what the job market actually asks for. This takes about 5–10 minutes.

Purpose of this survey: to find out which technical skills BSIT and BSCS students are learning in each subject, and how far (Introduced, Practiced or Applied). UEnvision compares this with the skills IT and Computer Science job postings ask for, to show the skill gaps in the curriculum and your own skill gap for the job you want.

How to answer

    {% if prefill %}
  1. Check the details from your registration under About you. If your year level or section has changed, press Edit to update it.
    2. {% else %}
  2. Read the Data Privacy Notice and tick the box to agree.
  3. Fill in your name, email, student number, course, year level and section.
    4. {% endif %}
  4. Your subjects appear after you pick your course and year level. Tick every subject you are taking this semester, and the ones you took last semester (use Select all to tick a whole semester at once). Subjects from earlier semesters or higher year levels are optional. For a Professional Elective, use + Add a subject that isn’t listed.
  5. For each subject you ticked, type or pick the skills you learned in it, then choose how far you got with each one: Introduced, Practiced or Applied (the same levels faculty use; each level is explained above the skill boxes). If a skill isn’t suggested, type it in the skill box to search for it. Press × on a subject's box to remove it.
  6. Press Submit survey. You then go straight to your dashboard to see your own skill gap results.

Data Privacy Notice

UEnvision is collecting this information for academic research purposes only, under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173).

  • Purpose: to compare the technical skills taught in the BSIT/BSCS curricula against current IT/CS job market demand.
  • What is collected: your name, email, course, student number, year level, section, the subjects you've taken, and the skills and levels you report for each.
  • Confidentiality: your responses are used only in aggregate for this research and are not shared outside the research team and its adviser.
  • Voluntary participation: you may decline to answer or stop at any time without consequence.
  • Questions or concerns: email the UEnvision research team at inquiries@uenvision.tech.
